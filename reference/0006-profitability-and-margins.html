The Three-Tier Margin Rule:
Gross Margin = (Revenue − COGS) ÷ Revenue
Operating Margin = (Gross Profit − Operating Expenses) ÷ Revenue
Net Margin = (Operating Profit − Interest − Taxes) ÷ Revenue

Agent-world reasoning guardrails

  • Margin percentage vs. Dollar profit: A high percentage margin on small sales produces less cash than a moderate margin on high volume. Always check total dollar contribution: Volume × (Price − Variable Cost).
  • Short-term vs. Long-term cuts: Cutting SG&A (e.g. slashing IT security, marketing, or employee benefits) instantly boosts operating margin today, but creates technical debt, attrition, and pipeline collapse tomorrow.
  • Breakeven check for campaigns: An incremental marketing expense of $X requires at least $X ÷ Gross Margin % in new revenue just to break even on operating profit.
