Working capital & the cash cycle.

Go to Lesson 05: Cash Conversion Cycle →

Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) = DIO + DSO − DPO
The time in days between paying cash for inventory and receiving cash from customer sales. It represents the gap where the business must finance its operations from cash reserves, debt, or equity.

The core working capital metrics

MetricNameCalculation / DefinitionWhat it measures
DIODays Inventory OutstandingAverage Inventory ÷ COGS × 365 (or days between arrival and shipment)How long inventory sits before being sold. Lower means faster stock turns; risk is stockout.
DSODays Sales OutstandingAverage Receivables ÷ Credit Sales × 365 (or days between delivery and collection)How long customers take to pay invoices. Lower means quicker cash inflow; risk is lost sales from harsh terms.
Operating CycleOperating CycleDIO + DSOThe total operational span from receiving goods to collecting cash from customers.
DPODays Payable OutstandingAverage Payables ÷ COGS × 365 (or days between invoice and payment)How long the company waits before paying suppliers. Higher delays cash outflow; risk is damaged vendor trust.
CCCCash Conversion CycleDIO + DSO − DPOThe net financing gap. When negative, suppliers finance the business before customers pay.

Departmental levers & trade-offs

DepartmentLeverShortens CCCDanger of single-metric over-optimization
OperationsInventory (DIO)Produce/order in smaller batches; lean inventoryStockouts, machine downtime, inability to fulfill sudden demand spikes
SalesReceivables (DSO)Tighter credit terms; aggressive collection callsB2B buyers switch to competitors who offer 30-day or 60-day terms
ProcurementPayables (DPO)Negotiate longer payment windows (e.g. 60 days)Suppliers raise prices, withhold volume discounts, or cut credit limits
FinanceTreasury & LiquidityBalance prompt discounts against funding costsEarly payments deplete cash reserves before customer collections arrive

Agent-world rules for working capital

  • Multi-objective balance: Do not let autonomous agents optimize working capital metrics in isolation. Enforce minimum safety stock days (Operations), competitive credit ranges (Sales), and supplier compliance terms (Procurement).
  • Contractual vs. actual: Separate agreed credit terms from observed historical days. A 30-day invoice that takes 45 days to collect indicates a credit risk or dispute, not an agreed 45-day financing plan.
  • Financing cost: Working capital is not free. When CCC is positive, calculate the carrying cost: Working Capital Needed × Cost of Capital.
