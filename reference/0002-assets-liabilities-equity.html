Cedar Desk Co. / Reference 02

A company's financial snapshot.

Assets = Liabilities + Equity
Equity = Assets − Liabilities.
All three are balances at a date, not activity over a period.
TermMeaningDo not confuse with
AssetA present economic resource controlled by the company due to past events. A resource is a right with potential economic benefits. Examples: cash, receivables, inventory.Only physical property, or only cash.
LiabilityA present obligation to transfer an economic resource due to past events. Examples: loan principal owed, supplier invoices payable.Only bank borrowing.
EquityResidual interest after deducting liabilities from assets.A bank balance or a company's market valuation.
InventoryHere, unsold desks held for resale, recorded at cost under the exercise's assumptions.An immediate expense just because cash was paid.
PayableAn amount the company owes, such as a supplier bill. It is a liability.A receivable: money owed to the company, an asset.
Balance sheetStatement of financial position: a dated snapshot of assets, liabilities, and equity.A statement of income and expenses over a period.

Transaction patterns

EventAsset effectLiability effectEquity effect

Receive resale inventory on supplier creditInventory ↑Supplier payable ↑No change
Collect an existing customer invoiceCash ↑, receivable ↓ by same amountNo changeNo change

Scope: simple transactions without fees, interest, taxes, write-downs, or other changes. Inventory qualifies for recognition and is worth at least cost. Normally inventory is measured at the lower of cost and net realizable value; losses can create expenses before sale. When it is sold, its carrying amount becomes an expense in the period of related revenue.

Agent check: model the whole transaction, not just the desired metric. Verify the accounting identity, then separately check classification, timing, permissions, and whether the decision serves the business.

Sources: IFRS Conceptual Framework summary, pp. 7–9; IAS 2 overview. Fictional transaction patterns apply these principles under the stated assumptions.
