Cedar Desk Co. / Course Notes

Course & Teaching Notes.

User-stated learning goals

  • Learn company and departmental measures (including ROI and current ratio) in business context, not as isolated formulas.
  • Learn top-down: company-wide outcomes, then departmental contributions and measures.
  • Emphasize dependencies between measures and effects on the business, people, and processes.
  • Cover Finance/Accounting, HR, Marketing, Sales, Operations, IT, Administration, Customer Service, Legal & Compliance, Procurement/Purchasing, and Product/Service Development (R&D).

Confirmed teaching preferences

  • Purpose: conceptual understanding to prepare a business world for AI agents.
  • Start from the basics; do not assume prior accounting knowledge.
  • Fictional company: Cedar Desk Co. designs, makes, and sells desks to businesses. Currency: illustrative dollars.
  • Introduce agent implications alongside business concepts, without letting implementation displace business learning.

Course Roadmap & Completed Milestones

  • Lesson 01: Revenue recognition upon transfer of control vs. cash collections.
  • Lesson 02: Assets, liabilities, equity, loan proceeds, and inventory purchases.
  • Lesson 03: Current ratio and its composition/timing limits.
  • Lesson 04: Dated cash timeline connecting Procurement, Operations, Sales, and Finance.
  • Lesson 05: Cash Conversion Cycle ($CCC = DIO + DSO - DPO$) and multi-departmental levers.
  • Lesson 06: Profitability & Margins (Gross, Operating, Net) across 11 departments.
  • Lesson 07: Returns on Capital (ROI and ROIC), DuPont decomposition, and hurdle rates.
  • Practice Room: Exercise Dashboard and 6 daily practice sets in exercises/.
  • Master Glossary: Comprehensive 39-term reference and 12-minute spoken audio guide with 5s pauses.
