Curated Notes & Limitations

  • Statutory reporting vs. operational models: Teaching models use explicit isolated assumptions to build intuition, rather than substituting for full jurisdictional GAAP/IFRS disclosures.
  • Empirical causality: Plausible dependencies between departmental actions (e.g. extending credit terms to boost sales) are treated as trade-offs and behavioral hypotheses, not immutable causal laws.
