Learning Framework

Why do these exercises?

  1. 01

    Desirable Difficulty

    Recognizing a formula in a lesson is easy; retrieving and applying it under realistic constraints creates durable mental models.

  2. 02

    Multi-Department Synthesis

    Real business problems never affect just one department. These exercises force you to evaluate decisions across Sales, Operations, Procurement, and Finance.

  3. 03

    Agent Guardrail Design

    Every problem highlights an unintended incentive or optimization trap that an autonomous AI agent would fall into if given naive objectives.
