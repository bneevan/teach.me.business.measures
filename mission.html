Cedar Desk Co. / Course Foundations

Mission: Business measures as a world for AI agents.

Why this course exists

Build conceptual business understanding to prepare a coherent business world in which AI agents can operate. Understand what departments do, how their measures connect to company outcomes, and how decisions affect people and processes—not merely how to calculate isolated ratios.

What success looks like

  • Explain company outcomes: Trace a fictional company's company-wide outcomes into departmental activities and measures.
  • Interpret measures in context: Master each measure's definition, period, inputs, limitations, and dependencies.
  • Trace cross-departmental decisions: Distinguish rigid accounting identities from uncertain causal effects, identifying trade-offs and unintended incentives.
  • Design agent guardrails: Describe agent objectives, observations, actions, and safety guardrails grounded in real business relationships.

Core constraints

  • Start from the basics; do not assume prior accounting background.
  • Use one connected fictional company (Cedar Desk Co.) and short, interactive lessons.
  • Cover all 11 core departments: Finance/Accounting, HR, Marketing, Sales, Operations, IT, Administration, Customer Service, Legal & Compliance, Procurement, and Product/Service Development.

Out of scope (for now)

Agent software implementation details and advanced corporate accounting are deferred while establishing fundamentals.
