How did recall go?

Make room for recall.

New and due cards appear first. “Not yet” schedules another review in 10 minutes. Successful recall schedules reviews after 1, 3, 7, then 14 days; further successes stay at 14 days. These are simple practice intervals, not a personalized retention model. Use “Review due cards” when you return; there are no notifications.

“Practice all cards” includes cards not yet due and also updates their schedules. These self-ratings guide practice only; they do not establish mastery.
