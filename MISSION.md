# Mission: Business measures as a world for AI agents

## Why
Build conceptual business understanding to prepare a coherent business world in which AI agents can operate. Understand what departments do, how their measures connect to company outcomes, and how decisions affect people and processes—not merely how to calculate isolated ratios.

## Success looks like
- Explain a fictional company's company-wide outcomes, then trace their drivers into departmental activities and measures.
- Interpret a measure's definition, period, inputs, limitations, and dependencies.
- Trace decisions across departments, distinguishing accounting identities from uncertain causal effects and identifying trade-offs and unintended incentives.
- Describe agent objectives, observations, actions, and guardrails grounded in those business relationships.

## Constraints
- Start from the basics; assume no accounting background.
- Use one fictional company and short, interactive lessons.
- Cover Finance/Accounting, HR, Marketing, Sales, Operations, IT, Administration, Customer Service, Legal & Compliance, Procurement, and Product/Service Development.

## Out of scope
- No explicit exclusions yet. Agent implementation and advanced accounting are deferred while establishing fundamentals.
