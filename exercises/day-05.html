Day 05: Capital Efficiency, ROI & ROIC

Instructions: Solve the three problems below evaluating Cedar Desk Co.'s return on invested capital and equipment investment options. When finished, paste your answers into the chat for review.

Key Concepts Tested: ROI vs. ROIC, Invested Capital measurement, The DuPont equation (Operating Margin × Capital Turnover = ROIC), Hurdle Rate / Cost of Capital, and the CapEx automation trap.

Problem 5.1: Measuring Company ROIC

Cedar Desk Co. closes its fiscal year with the following financial snapshot:

  • Annual Sales Revenue: $400,000
  • Annual Operating Profit (EBIT): $60,000
  • Factory Equipment & Building Improvements: $180,000
  • Operating Working Capital (Inventory + Receivables − Payables): $70,000
  • Cash in Bank (Checking Account): $20,000

Questions to answer:

  1. Calculate Cedar's Total Invested Capital (Fixed Operating Assets + Operating Working Capital).
  2. Calculate Cedar's Return on Invested Capital (ROIC):
    ROIC = Operating Profit ÷ Invested Capital
  3. If Cedar's investors require a minimum 12% return (the hurdle rate), is Cedar creating or destroying shareholder value?

Problem 5.2: The DuPont Decomposition (Margin × Velocity)

Use Cedar's numbers from Problem 5.1 ($400,000 Revenue, $60,000 Operating Profit, $250,000 Invested Capital).

Questions to answer:

  1. Calculate Cedar's Operating Margin %:
    Operating Margin = Operating Profit ÷ Revenue
  2. Calculate Cedar's Capital Turnover (Asset Velocity):
    Capital Turnover = Revenue ÷ Invested Capital
  3. Multiply your Operating Margin by your Capital Turnover:
    ROIC = Operating Margin × Capital Turnover
    Verify whether this product matches the ROIC you calculated in Problem 5.1.

Problem 5.3: The Capital Allocation Dilemma

Cedar's board of directors is evaluating two proposals to launch a new product line: Custom Solid Oak Conference Tables.

  • Option A (Handcrafted Workshop):
    • Equipment investment (Capital): $10,000 in manual precision saws and chisels
    • Annual Operating Profit generated: $4,000
  • Option B (Automated Robotic CNC Mill):
    • Equipment investment (Capital): $100,000 in computerized CNC carving machinery
    • Annual Operating Profit generated: $12,000 (higher volume, lower unit labor)

Questions to answer:

  1. Calculate the ROIC for Option A and Option B.
  2. Which option produces more absolute profit in dollars? Which option is a more efficient user of capital?
  3. Suppose Cedar must fund the entire equipment cost by borrowing from a commercial bank at an 8% annual interest rate:
    • What is Option A's annual loan interest expense, and what is its net profit after interest?
    • What is Option B's annual loan interest expense, and what is its net profit after interest?
    • Option B required $90,000 more debt than Option A ($100k vs $10k). How much additional net profit in dollars did that extra $90,000 in debt produce for the owners?
